# Meeting: 29/10/2026 (CRM Feature Planning)

## Salesperson View

* Company creation fields defined:
  * Name, owner, industry type, city, employee count, annual revenue, timezone, website, LinkedIn, expected team size
* Contact management:
  * Email, phone, owner, position, role, notes section
  * Contacts linked to companies; company affiliation updatable if a contact switches
* Deal creation mirrors HubSpot:
  * Deal name, pipeline, stage, amount
  * Stages: appointment booking, presentation scheduling, deal finalizing, finance contract, customer success onboarding
* Presentation stage triggers automatic notification to the presentation team
* Salesperson can only edit companies assigned to them, not other reps' accounts

## Salesperson Activity and Task Features

* Past activity timeline per company: full history of contact, conversation progress, last interaction
* Task section per contact:
  * Salesperson can self-add tasks
  * Sales lead can assign tasks to reps
  * Future: AI auto-generates tasks from meeting recordings
* Data visible to salesperson:
  * How long a company has used the product, their feedback, response patterns
  * For new customers: marketing form interactions and engagement history

## Notifications and Automation

* Deadline reminders: automatic alert when a task deadline approaches
* Email follow-up automation:
  * If no reply after 10 days, CRM auto-notifies the rep to follow up
  * Applies to both replied and non-replied outreach threads

## Sales Lead View and Additional Features

* Sales lead has full overview: all deals, all companies, which rep is assigned to which
* Can reassign reps across deals
* Comment feature: sales lead can leave inline comments on a deal for the assigned rep
* Calendar integration (Arjun): CRM tasks sync to an external calendar view
* AI summarization (Arjun): summarizes past calls and emails for a rep taking over a deal mid-stream
* Gamification dashboard (good-to-have, not must-have):
  * Monthly per-rep stats: calls made, conversions, deals confirmed
  * Leaderboard for top performers; tracks both sales volume and outreach effort separately
